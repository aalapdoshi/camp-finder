Privacy

Last updated October 11, 2026.

A2CampFinder is a way for Ann Arbor families to look through a community summer camp list, save camps, and sketch a summer plan. This note says what we keep about you.

The camp list

Camp names, ages, prices, locations, and schedules on this site come from a community spreadsheet. That information is shown publicly. It is not connected to your account.

Your account

You can browse without signing in. If you create an account, you sign in with Google. We receive the name and email address Google shares with us. We do not keep a password for you.

What you save

When you are signed in, we store:

  • Camps you mark as favorites
  • Summer plan entries: the camp, the dates, whether you marked it booked or want to book, a note if you add one, a cost if you type one in, and a child first name if you type one in so you can tell siblings apart

Your favorites and summer plan are visible to you when you are signed in. We do not publish them.

Calendar emails

If you choose to email yourself a calendar invite, we send it to the email address on your account. The invite includes the camp name and times, and the child first name if you entered one. We send it only when you ask.

Feedback

The feedback button saves a star rating, any comment you type, and which page you were on. That submission is not attached to your account.

How we see site use

We use Google Analytics to see which pages people visit. That can include the page, the browser, and a general location. It does not include your summer plan or a child’s name.

Who helps us run the site

  • Supabase stores accounts, favorites, and summer plans
  • Google handles sign-in
  • Google Analytics measures page visits
  • Airtable stores the camp list and feedback
  • Resend sends calendar emails you request
  • Netlify hosts the site

We do not sell this information.

Removing your information

Email a2summercamp@umich.edu and ask us to delete your account. We will delete your login, favorites, and summer plan. A feedback comment is not tied to your name, so include what you wrote if you want that removed too.

Questions

Email a2summercamp@umich.edu.
